# CrimeKit Product Research & Solution Document

**Version:** 1.0

## Executive Summary

CrimeKit is an AI-powered digital investigation platform designed to unify evidence management, forensic analysis, knowledge graphs, semantic search, workflow automation, and court-ready reporting into one secure ecosystem.

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Modern investigations generate massive amounts of digital evidence from mobile devices, computers, cloud services, CCTV, emails, chats, and social media.

Most organizations still rely on multiple disconnected tools, spreadsheets, and manual workflows, resulting in slow investigations, duplicated effort, inconsistent reporting, and increased risk of missing critical evidence.

---

# 2. Root Cause Analysis

## Technical
- Disconnected forensic tools
- Siloed databases
- Limited search capabilities
- No unified AI assistance

## Operational
- Manual evidence tracking
- Repetitive report preparation
- Complex chain-of-custody management

## Organizational
- Collaboration across teams is difficult
- Inconsistent investigation procedures
- Knowledge loss between cases

## Legal
- Audit trails may be incomplete
- Evidence integrity verification is time-consuming

# 4. Existing Approach

Common workflows involve separate forensic software, spreadsheets, shared drives, manual reports, and independent search systems.

## Limitations

- Limited automation
- Poor scalability
- Fragmented evidence lifecycle

# 6. Proposed Solution

CrimeKit integrates:

- Secure evidence management
- AI investigation assistant
- Semantic retrieval (pgvector)
- Knowledge graph (Neo4j)
- Full-text search (Elasticsearch)
- Workflow automation
- Timeline visualization
- Court-ready reporting
- Audit logging
- Enterprise security
