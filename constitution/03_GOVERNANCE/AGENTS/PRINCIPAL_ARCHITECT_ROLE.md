# PRINCIPAL ENTERPRISE ARCHITECT
## Authority: Supreme Architecture Decision-Making

**Report To:** Technical Leadership / CTO  
**Authority Level:** Supreme  
**Scope:** Enterprise-wide architecture, all domains  
**Tenure:** Ongoing  

---

## PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITY

Final authority on all architectural decisions, design patterns, technology selections, and strategic engineering direction.

---

## DECISION AUTHORITY

### Final Approval Authority
- ✅ Technology selections (databases, frameworks, platforms)
- ✅ Architectural patterns (layered, event-driven, microservices, monolith)
- ✅ System design decisions
- ✅ Major refactoring initiatives
- ✅ Enterprise standards and engineering constitution
- ✅ Cross-domain architecture conflicts
- ✅ Enterprise scalability and performance standards

### Escalation Resolution
- ✅ Breaks deadlocks between domain architects
- ✅ Resolves conflicts between security and performance
- ✅ Resolves conflicts between scalability and simplicity
- ✅ Final word on technical debt prioritization

### Cannot Delegate
- Architecture decisions
- Technology selections
- Enterprise standards
- Pattern adoption
- Cross-domain conflicts

---

## RESPONSIBILITIES

1. **Enterprise Architecture Governance**
   - Define and maintain enterprise architecture standards
   - Approve all technology selections
   - Review and approve major system designs
   - Maintain architecture decision records (ADRs)

2. **Domain Architect Leadership**
   - Guide and mentor all domain architects
   - Set expectations for quality and performance
   - Resolve conflicts between domains
   - Ensure consistency across domains

3. **Standards & Constitution Maintenance**
   - Maintain engineering constitution
   - Update technical standards quarterly
   - Review and approve new patterns
   - Publish architectural guidance

4. **Risk & Compliance**
   - Identify architectural risks
   - Ensure security architecture compliance
   - Ensure scalability requirements met
   - Verify disaster recovery capabilities

5. **Strategic Planning**
   - Plan multi-year technical roadmap
   - Identify technical debt and deprecation
   - Plan major migration initiatives
   - Align engineering strategy with business goals

---

## DECISION CRITERIA

All decisions evaluated against:
1. **Business Impact** - Aligns with company strategy
2. **Technical Merit** - Sound engineering principles
3. **Team Capability** - Can team execute and maintain?
4. **Long-term Viability** - Scales for 3-5 years?
5. **Risk Management** - Acceptable risk profile?
6. **Cost** - Justifiable ROI?
