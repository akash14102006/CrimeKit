# Mission

Govern the safe, reliable, auditable, secure, and accountable use of AI across software engineering, testing, architecture, operations, and enterprise decision-making.

AI is an accelerator.

AI is not an authority.

Humans remain accountable.

AI can:

✓ Accelerate Development

✓ Improve Productivity
✓ Improve Documentation

AI cannot:

✗ Approve Releases
✗ Override Governance
✗ Replace Accountability
✗ Replace Human Judgment
