## PURPOSE

---

## APPLICABLE REGULATIONS

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
**Applies to:** EU personal data  
**Requirements:**
- ✅ Explicit consent for data processing
- ✅ Right to access personal data
- ✅ Right to be forgotten
- ✅ Data breach notification (72 hours)
- ✅ Privacy impact assessment
- ✅ Data Protection Officer (if applicable)

### CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
**Applies to:** California resident data  
**Requirements:**
- ✅ Privacy policy
- ✅ Right to know personal data
- ✅ Right to delete personal data
- ✅ Right to opt-out of sales
- ✅ Non-discrimination for exercising rights

### HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act)
**Applies to:** Protected health information  
**Requirements:**
- ✅ Access controls
- ✅ Audit controls
- ✅ Integrity controls
- ✅ Transmission security
- ✅ Business associate agreements

### PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
**Applies to:** Payment card data  
**Requirements:**
- ✅ Network security
- ✅ Secure development practices
- ✅ Access control
- ✅ Testing and monitoring
- ✅ Information security policy

### SOC 2 (Service Organization Control)
**Applies to:** Service organizations  
**Requirements:**
- ✅ Security controls
- ✅ Availability controls
- ✅ Processing integrity
- ✅ Confidentiality controls
- ✅ Privacy controls

## DATA CLASSIFICATION

### Classification Levels

| Level | Examples | Handling | Encryption | Access |
|-------|----------|----------|-----------|--------|
| PUBLIC | Product info, marketing | No restrictions | Optional | Unrestricted |
| INTERNAL | Business data | Internal only | Optional | Employees |
| CONFIDENTIAL | Customer data, strategy | Restricted | Required | Limited |
| RESTRICTED | PII, payment data, health | Highly restricted | Required | Very limited |
