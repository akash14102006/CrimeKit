## 🔍 SEARCHING EFFECTIVELY

### By Technology
- **Backend:** Search for "backend", "Node.js", "NestJS"
- **Frontend:** Search for "React", "TypeScript", "component"
- **Database:** Search for "database", "PostgreSQL", "schema"
- **DevOps:** Search for "deployment", "infrastructure", "DevOps"
- **Security:** Search for "security", "authentication", "encryption"

### By Problem Type
- **"How do I..."** → Look in INSTRUCTIONS or SKILLS
- **"What are the standards?"** → Look in RULES or POLICIES
- **"What's the best approach?"** → Look in PATTERNS or EXAMPLES
- **"Who decides?"** → Look in WHICH_AGENT_TO_USE
- **"Step by step?"** → Look in WORKFLOWS
- **"I need a template"** → Look in TEMPLATES

### By Document Type
- **Workflows:** Process documents with phases and gates
- **Rules:** Enforcement constraints (must follow)
- **Skills:** Learning and expertise documentation
- **Patterns:** Proven architectural approaches
- **Templates:** Ready-to-use code/document templates
- **Examples:** Complete working implementations
- **Checklists:** Quality assurance step-by-step
- **Policies:** Governance and enforcement frameworks
